title: "Teams"
description: "Create a team, invite people, run under it, and see each other's work."
---

Every account has a personal organization from the start. A team is an organization you create and invite people into: jobs, checks, sessions, datasets, agents and skills belong to one organization, and every member sees them.

```bash
evolve auth org create acme --display-name "Acme Lab"
evolve auth org invite acme
```

`invite` prints a token once. Whoever runs `evolve auth org join <token>` while signed in joins as a member. Owners manage members and invites; members run and read.

## Run under a team

```bash
evolve run -d terminal-bench-4@4.0 -a claude -m haiku -l 5 --org acme
evolve job list --scope org
```

`--org` names the organization a job lands in. Without it, the job goes to your default organization (`evolve auth org use acme` sets one, `evolve auth org use personal` clears it) or to your personal one. `dataset publish --org` places a new dataset the same way; a new version of an existing dataset stays where that dataset is. The public catalog is visible to every organization. `job list --scope` picks what you see: `my` (yours), `shared` (what teammates created, and runs shared with you by email) or `org` (everything in your organizations, yours included).

In the SDK the same choice is `org: "acme"` on `jobs().start()` and `datasets().publish()`, or once for a client with `hosted({ apiKey, org: "acme" })`.

## In the dashboard

The box under your account in the sidebar is the workspace switcher. It always names one workspace: the one in the page's URL, otherwise the one you last opened in this tab, otherwise Personal. Its menu lists Personal and every team you belong to, each with your role, and **Create team**. Pick a workspace and Datasets, Jobs, Traces, Members and Settings show that workspace's work; on a page that is yours alone (Skills, Integrations, Secrets, API Keys, Usage, Billing) the pick is remembered for the next one.

TEAM in the sidebar has two pages. Members lists people with their role: an owner has MAKE OWNER, DEMOTE and REMOVE on every other row, and everyone has LEAVE on their own; below the list, an owner has NEW INVITE LINK, shown once, and REVOKE. A person who opens an invite link signs in, or signs up, and joins. Settings holds the team's name, and DELETE TEAM for an owner. In Personal, Members lists only you and Settings shows your workspace's name.

Traces is under the workspace too. In a team it is one list of every run in that team, with a CREATED BY column naming who ran each. In Personal it has two tabs: MINE (yours, in any team) and SHARED WITH ME (what teammates made, plus runs shared with you by email). Skills lists your own uploads.
